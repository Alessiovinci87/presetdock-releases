# PresetDock — preset manager for Line 6 Helix

**PresetDock is a Windows app that turns the Helix presets you already own into one searchable library.** Point it at your folders of `.hlx` presets, `.hls` setlists and Helix Stadium `.hsp` files, and find any tone in seconds — by sound, amp, cab, effect or artist, or by describing it in plain words.

**[presetdock.com](https://presetdock.com/en/)** · [FAQ](https://presetdock.com/en/faq) · [Guides](https://presetdock.com/en/guides) · [Pricing](https://presetdock.com/en/pricing)

## What it does

- **One library for every preset** — single presets, setlists (each preset inside becomes searchable) and Helix Stadium presets.
- **Search by sound** — clean, edge of breakup, crunch, lead, high gain, ambient; filter by amp, cab, effect, artist or IR use.
- **Ask in plain language** — "U2-style delay" or "British crunch with little reverb": the AI assistant suggests three presets from *your* library and explains why.
- **Build your board** — drag presets onto banks and slots (1A–32D), keep one board per gig or band, export a ready `.hls` setlist for HX Edit. For HX Stomp and Stomp XL it exports numbered single presets.
- **Open in HX Edit** with a double-click, or drag the file out.
- **Spots duplicates** and the IR slots a preset needs.

## Good to know

- **Windows 10 and 11.** No Mac version yet.
- **Your files stay on your computer.** PresetDock never sells, shares or uploads presets. The AI assistant receives your question and a technical description of candidate presets, never the files.
- **14-day full trial**, then a one-time licence: EUR 24.99 (EUR 19.99 at launch) with 100 AI searches included. VAT included.
- Works with presets made for Helix Floor, LT, Rack, Native, HX Stomp, HX Stomp XL, HX Effects and Helix Stadium. POD Go presets use a different format and are not supported.
